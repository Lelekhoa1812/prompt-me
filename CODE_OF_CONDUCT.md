# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We aim to maintain an inclusive, professional, and constructive project environment for users and contributors. Treat participants with dignity regardless of identity, background, experience, or disagreement.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and evidence-based when reviewing ideas or code.
- Assume good intentions while being candid about impact and defects.
- Welcome questions and constructive feedback.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, and differing experience levels.
- Keep contributions and reviews focused on the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, intimidation, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberately disruptive conduct, publishing another person's private data without permission, and threats are not acceptable.

## Scope and moderation

These expectations apply to project issues, discussions, PRs, and other official project spaces. Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove inappropriate material or restrict participation when necessary.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through a private GitHub contact method when available. For security-sensitive matters, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not post sensitive personal allegations publicly. The project currently has no dedicated moderation team or response-time guarantee.

This is a project-specific conduct policy, not a representation that the project is governed by an external organisation.
